# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (2026-10-26)

First release. 17 tools:

- TCMB exchange rates (latest and historical since 1996) and currency conversion
- Recent earthquakes from AFAD and Kandilli Observatory
- Public holidays 2024-2035 (religious holidays from Diyanet's official tables, half-day eves included), date check, add/count business days
- 81 provinces and 973 districts (offline)
- TCKN, VKN and Turkish IBAN validation with bank lookup (offline)
- Prayer times (Diyanet method, via Aladhan) and weather (Open-Meteo)
- Turkish-aware upper/lower/title case, ASCII and slugs; amounts in Turkish words ("yazıyla")
